## What this says
- **Quick, already-existing gains (about 14B):** math, agent trajectories, scientific Q&A, biology, earth, scientific discovery, chemistry, scientific programming and code are limited by the caps we chose, not by the raw data (if the 20 Oct estimates hold). Each is one rebuild with `build_dataset.py` and a check.
- **Needs new collection:** physics, engineering reasoning, the three Namit domains, domains 9 and 10. They have no known larger source.
- **Raju, 1 Nov: medical is in but at low weight (target 1.0B, mix share about 3%); priority goes to generative engineering (11), experimental validation (12), technical communication (14) and the other most required domains.** The unique corpus is then about 29B, and only if every estimate above holds; the three Namit domains need new collection to reach their targets.
- **Raju, 1 Nov (DM): give more priority to the other scientific domains such as math and physics, which come under the same zone.** Consequence in this plan: math (already 1.0B, raw 9.3B) is the largest and cheapest gain, so its rebuild goes first; physics has only 0.64B and no larger source, so new physics collection (arXiv physics, textbooks, the physics part of Swaraj's domains 9 and 10) goes to the front of the collection queue. Targets below are unchanged until Raju gives numbers; math 4.0B and physics 1.2B are my proposal, medical stays at 1.0B.
- **The remainder can be repetition.** Published scaling studies of data-constrained training report that up to about 4 passes over the same data is close to as good as new data; 26B unique tokens covers 30B with 1.15 passes. Balance matters more than the headline number: in this table math plus biology is about a quarter of the corpus; without caps they would dominate.
- **Not in the plan:** raw Common Crawl text (not verified science), unchecked uploads, anything without a licence note.

## Rules for every new bin (unchanged)
Frozen tokenizer `vocab32k_v2`; whole-file `check_bins` PASS; the document-boundary rule stated in the build config and checked by mean document length; exact-duplicate removal; a 20 MB source sample sent first; train and validation never from the same document; the build manifest kept next to the bin; licence and source recorded.
