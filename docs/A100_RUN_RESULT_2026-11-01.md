## Why `val_loss` is null in `sample_outputs.json`
`scripts/sample_outputs.py` copies `val_loss` from the checkpoint's metadata. `ckpt_last.pt` is written every 200 steps (8,200) and validation runs every 250 (8,000, 8,250), so the step-8,200 checkpoint carries no validation value. It is a reporting gap, not a failed evaluation; the real value is in `metrics.jsonl` (2.166 at step 8,250). Fix: make `sample_outputs.py` compute the mixed validation loss itself, or take it from `metrics.jsonl`.

## Capability probes and held-out continuations (read: sample_outputs, step 8,200, greedy decoding)
- Next-token top-1 accuracy on 81,920 held-out tokens per domain: math 43.6%, physics 41.0%, gen_eng 49.7%, exp_val 41.6%, tech_comm 38.9%, code 66.6%, chem 49.4%; mean 47.2%.
- Fixed probes: **2 of 10 pass** (`def add` and the chemical formula of water). Fails: 17 x 24, 125 + 379, derivative of x^2, factorial, Newton's second law, speed of light, DNA bases, rock types.
- 9 of 14 held-out continuations flagged "repetition loop". Decoding is greedy (`temperature=0.0`, no repetition penalty), which makes small base models loop even when they are right about the topic (measured below: sampling removes about 60% of the loops).
- Good cases: the apache licence header and the patent boilerplate are continued almost exactly (match of the first 8 tokens 8/8). Bad cases: arithmetic, definitions, and any continuation that needs new facts.

## What this does and does not show
- Is: a clean, healthy pretraining run of a small model at about 21 tokens per parameter (2.15B / ~0.1B), stopped before the end of its cosine schedule.
- Is not: a measurement of what the 1.5B model will do. At 100M parameters and 2B tokens, failing arithmetic and facts is the expected outcome for a base model without instruction data (expectation from the scaling literature, not measured here).
- Not done: a held-out arithmetic/maths probe set larger than the 10 fixed probes; evaluation at the training context length (2,048).

## Greedy versus sampled decoding (read: my run on `ckpt_best.pt`, 10 probes and 14 held-out continuations)
| setting | probes passed (regex) | probes that loop | held-out continuations that loop |
|---|---|---|---|
| greedy | 3 / 10 | 4 | 8 / 14 |
| temperature 0.7, top-p 0.9 (seed 0) | 3 / 10 | 0 | 3 / 14 |
| temperature 0.7, top-p 0.9 (seed 1) | 3 / 10 | 1 | 3 / 14 |
So about 60% of the looping is the decoding (8 to 3 of 14); the rest is the model. The regex "pass" overstates correctness: the derivative probe "passes" in greedy mode because the text contains "2x" inside a wrong formula, and Newton's law "passes" on the word "force" in a wrong sentence. **Truly correct: only `def add`** (and the water formula under one sampling seed). Arithmetic (17 x 24, 125 + 379) fails in every setting.
